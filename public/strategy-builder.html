    Before real-money trading becomes available

    The video uses three modes: historical backtest, forward paper validation and broker live execution. Rangeform currently exposes the first two as research/paper tools; this switch intentionally opens a read-only live market monitor, not a brokerage trading engine. We must verify broker eligibility, market-data licensing, secure authentication, position reconciliation and kill switches before enabling real-money execution.

    An EMA backtest on the underlying is not a NIFTY options backtest. Options require dated strike/expiry premium series, taxes and market-specific settlement costs. This tool never places real or paper broker orders.
