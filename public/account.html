INSTALL RANGEFORM

In Safari open Rangeform, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App, and tap Add. Launch Rangeform from the new Home Screen icon to remove the browser address and navigation bars. Normal browser tabs cannot hide Safari or Chrome controls.
