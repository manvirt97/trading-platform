Why are the controls disabled? Complete owner setup
  1. Sign in with Google on Account & Sign-in. Copy your Firebase UID from the “Your owner ID for Cloudflare” card near the top.
  2. Open Cloudflare → Workers & Pages → rangeform. In Production, set BOT_OWNER_UID to your Firebase UID and BOT_SERVICE_ENABLED=on. Confirm the D1 binding named DB exists and redeploy Pages.
  3. Deploy the separately configured rangeform-autonomous-paper-bot scheduled Worker with its own D1 binding and owner secret, as shown in the exact deployment guide.
  4. Enable the paper strategy, run one manual verification tick, then confirm CRON AUTONOMY → RECENT WORKER TICK advances while the browser is closed. A manual tick alone does not prove autonomy.

Real-money order execution is deliberately not connected. Never enter exchange or broker credentials in this page.
