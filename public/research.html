UNDERSTANDING THE MODEL

What does an alignment score mean?

It is a simple 0–100 indicator of agreement among historical technical rules. It is not the chance a trade will win, a forecast, or an expected return.

A higher score means more of these rules are positive: trend relative to moving averages, recent momentum, 20-bar range and (only if available) volume.

Timing matters: a reading based on old bars cannot be used as a live day-trading signal. Always check the data age, spread, liquidity and news independently.
